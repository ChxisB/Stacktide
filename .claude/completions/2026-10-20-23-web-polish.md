# 23 web-polish - done 2026-10-20

Every screen made spacious and tidy after reading the real pages (dark and light, 1280, 1440 and 390 px). It changes spacing, table layout, the weight of buttons and a few labels. It changes no behaviour and no API. Nothing is committed.

## What was built
- **PLS-1** One rhythm for cards and fields: a card's children have no margin of their own and sit one gap (20 px) apart, cards have 28 px padding, `.stack` gap is 24 px, `h3` is a section start with a rule above it, inputs and selects stop at 30 rem and textareas do not.
- **PLS-2** Tables that keep whole words: row headers break only at word boundaries, `time` never wraps, 16 px cell padding, compact (32 px) quiet buttons inside a table.
- **PLS-3** Retry (Status), Dismiss (Findings) and Test (Settings) show one word; the full name stays for assistive technology in a visually hidden span, so every test that finds them by name passes unchanged.
- **PLS-4** The Variables cell: value, tags, then a row of quiet words (Delete in the danger colour), top-aligned with its row header.
- **PLS-5** Home checklist shows the link with its hint under it; Sync with no targets says so inside a card; the Projects connection row aligns its button; the wizard steps and forms get the card rhythm.
- **PLS-6** Read all ten routes. Fixed: the Status actions cell (`.row.nowrap`, so a row with a Retry is no taller than one without), the token form rule that now spans its card, the Pipeline choice cards (title and hint 4 px apart, not a stack's 24).
- **PLS-7** `/code-review` of the diff: three small fixes (below), the rest logged.
