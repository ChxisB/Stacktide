## 2026-10-20 - DNS-10 Lint rules

Rules and severities: `spf-single` (error: two or more SPF records on one name, found in any case and across split strings; `v=spf10` or a TXT that only mentions SPF is not one), `dmarc-with-mx` (warn: a name with a real MX, not a null MX of RFC 7505, and neither its own `_dmarc.<name>` nor a zone-apex `_dmarc`; one finding per name), `caa-apex` (warn: no CAA at `@`), `ttl-range` (warn: a TTL under 60 or over 86400, one finding per record name and type; the bounds themselves pass), `keep-unknown` (error: the zone has no `keepunknown` while the stack does not prune; none when it prunes).
